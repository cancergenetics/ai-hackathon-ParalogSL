## Notes on how keys were chosen

- fig2: categories assigned by the ordered keyword map below (first match wins) applied to the lower-cased `description`; `opus (inherited)` rows are Opus agents whose model was inherited from the lead session and are drawn hollow.
- fig4: `T_perfold_vs_B1` = T_pf (mean per-fold paired AP gain over B1); human stratum-averaged AP = `human_strat_avg_ap/mean`; guard line = `results/B1/human_strat_avg_ap/mean` (equals `incumbent_human_strat_avg_ap`). Filled markers = `selection/<family>/selected_passes_guard`. 'Anchored' = the rank-ensemble-with-B1 candidates (`LR_ENS_sel`, `GBDT_ENS_sel`) of the ablation file; the other ablation candidates (SIM, SIMFAM, SIMGENE, MONO) are not drawn. 'Deconfounded' = `LRdc_sel`, `GBDTdc_sel`. B0, B3, B4 and the leave-one-species-out runs are not drawn.
- fig5: test-set AP, prevalence and gains for the pairs scored by the caller (primary population).
- fig6: `ci()` reads `point`, `lo`, `hi` of each `delta_vs_B1` block; row labels give the number of positives (`n_pos`). 'Human, trained on both' and 'Yeast, trained on both' use the frozen model's entry in the cross-species blocks.
- fig7: importance = `mean_drop_ap` (held-out permutation importance: fall in AP when the feature is shuffled), whiskers = `sd` over `n` = 25 seed x fold splits. Top 10 = highest mean of the human and yeast `mean_drop_ap` among the 73 single features (ALL_TIER_A / ALL_TIER_B shown separately). Features shown: `other_gap_max`, `rank_max_pct`, `other_gap_min`, `tb_rel_rate`, `tb_cons_min_pct`, `tb_n_post_single`, `lowcomp_frac_max`, `ident_global`, `ident_ens_min`, `tb_dupability_min`.
- fig8: panel a uses `T_perfold_vs_B1` of each condition; panel b uses per-fold mean AP of the model and B1 (`ap_*_natural`, `ap_*_degraded`).
